The game

A storm throws your boat onto the reefs of a cursed island in the Pacific. All you have is the journal of Steven Smith, an explorer who came here in 1934 looking for a priceless diamond hidden by a vanished civilisation, and never left. Find the diamond, and get off the island alive.

The Diamond of Cursed Island is an unofficial, fully illustrated remake of Le Diamant de l'Île Maudite, the 1984 Oric adventure by Bertrand d'Armagnac and Frédéric Baille (Loriciels). You see the island through your own eyes, in four directions from each of its 49 places, and you act by giving orders, as in the original. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, in English and French.

Giving orders

Orders are a verb and a noun: TAKE CLUB, OPEN DRAWER, THROW KNIFE. FORWARD walks 50 metres the way you face; LEFT, RIGHT and BACK turn you. LOOK, EXAMINE, INVENTORY, STATUS, REST and HELP are there when you need them. The game knows about 90 words in each language.

Choose how the game reads your orders with Commands in the menu:

  • Short form (as in 1984, the default): a word is recognised from its first three or four letters and completed as you type, so THR CLU becomes THROW CLUB. A word the game doesn't know is erased with a beep. Chain up to 40 orders with /: FORWARD/LEFT/TAKE GOURD.
  • Long form: whole sentences, with other verb forms and little words, such as "pick up the coconut", "throw the club at the spider" or "take the rope and the bucket then go forward". Chain orders with and, then, a comma or /. The short form's abbreviations still work.

On a phone or tablet you don't need to type: tap WORDS, then a verb, then a noun, and the order is given. A keyboard works too.

The island

  • Two parts. Part one is the island's surface: the beach and the wreck, the jungle, a ruined tower, an explorer's hut, a swamp, the volcano and an old military hangar. Part two is underground: a temple, galleries, a well, and the creatures that live there.
  • Look everywhere. You only see what is in front of you, and some things can be seen from one place, facing one way. Turn round in every new place.
  • You can only take what you see, and you can't carry more than eight things.
  • Survival. Hunger, thirst, fatigue, fever, injuries and venom add up, as in the original. When your FITNESS bar reaches zero, you die. STATUS tells you what is wrong. Time passes even while you think, so don't wait too long.
  • Saving. The game is saved after every order; Continue game on the menu picks up where you left off. The start of part two is a checkpoint: if you die underground, choose Restart part two.
  • Score. Each step of the quest earns points, up to 100. The Hall of Fame ranks you by score, then by the fewest moves.

Questions

The game erases my words.

In the short form, a word the game doesn't know is erased with a beep, as in 1984. Try another word, or switch to the long form under Commands in the menu. HELP and the Instructions show the kind of orders the game understands; on a touch screen, WORDS lists them all.

I keep dying.

Look after yourself: eat, drink, rest and treat your wounds, and check STATUS often. Some creatures must be dealt with before you get close, and some places are dangerous without the right equipment. The original was famously hard; the checkpoint at the start of part two is there to help.

I'm stuck.

Turn round in every place: many things are only visible from one direction. EXAMINE what you find and read what you can. Something that seems useless may hide something else.

How do I change the language?

Choose Language in the menu. The first time, the game follows your device's language.

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device, in the game's own storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Privacy

The Diamond of Cursed Island collects nothing. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it never connects to the internet. It doesn't collect, store, share or sell any personal information, from anyone, including children.

The game keeps a few small files in its private storage on your device: your settings (language, command form and volume), your saved game and the part two checkpoint, and the Hall of Fame (the names you enter, with your scores and moves). They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
